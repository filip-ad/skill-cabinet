# Product

## Register

product

## Users
A single operator with private Tailnet access. They have agent skills across several command-line tools and source machines. They open the cabinet to see the governed catalog, read a skill, and inspect its use and currency evidence.

## Product Purpose
Skill Cabinet is a private, read-only catalog for installed agent skills. Success is being able to find a skill quickly, understand its approved text and metadata, and compare source machines without exposing raw agent logs.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, technical archive. The interface uses the operator-approved dark catalog treatment. Confidence comes from order, density, and legibility, not decoration. The operator approved the rendered grouped-skill dashboard in chat on 2026-09-16.

## Anti-references
Marketing dashboards. Decorative neon effects. Identical icon-and-caption card grids. Glassmorphism. Cute confirmations for destructive actions.

## Design Principles
1. The skill body is the object: give it a readable measure and keep chrome out of the way.
2. Filters stay compact. The skill list gets most of the working space. Metadata is cataloguing, not a settings panel.
3. The cabinet is read-only. Skill lifecycle work stays in governed agent sessions.
4. Density is allowed in the drawers; emptiness belongs to the reading surface.
5. Every label should sound like a library, not a startup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast. No additional motion or colour-blindness requirements beyond that.
